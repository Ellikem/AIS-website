Applying To AIS

The Adwenpa International School Admissions Team is here to guide and support your family throughout the entire application and enrolment process. We are committed to making this process as easy as possible while we create an exciting welcoming experience for your family.

Important Dates

1
Registration Opens

June 1, 2026

2
Registration Closes

August 15, 2026

3
Application Review

August 16 - September 10, 2026

4
Admission Decisions

September 15, 2026

5
Enrollment Deadline

October 1, 2026

1. Online Inquiry

Begin by submitting an online inquiry form with your child’s details. Our admissions team will review your request and follow up within 24–48 hours.

2. Application

Complete the official application form and upload required documents, including transcripts, ID, and any recommendation letters.

3. Financial Aid

If you wish to apply for financial support, submit supporting financial documents at the same time as your application.

4. Admission Decision

Our team will evaluate your application and notify you of the admission decision via email and phone.

5. Enrollment

Once accepted, confirm your place by completing the enrollment form and paying any required fees.

6. Deposit

Secure your child’s place with the required deposit by the deadline. This finalizes the admission process.

Have Any Questions ?...

If you would like to learn more about tuition, payment plans, or financial aid, our admissions advisors are here to help. Reach out to us for personalized support and guidance.

At Adwenpa International School, we are committed to providing quality education that empowers students to reach their full potential.
