Academic Curriculum

Building Skills for the real world

At Adwenpa International School, we are committed to providing a comprehensive and dynamic curriculum that prepares our students for success in the real world. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills, while also promoting a love for learning. We offer a wide range of subjects, including core academic disciplines, as well as arts, technology, and physical education. Our experienced educators work closely with students to ensure they receive a well-rounded education that equips them with the knowledge and skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing global landscape.

Three Pathways, One Future

Our academic system is organized into three distinct pathways: the Vocational Pathway, the Technological Pathway, and the Media Pathway. Each pathway is designed to cater to the unique needs and aspirations of our students, providing them with a clear and structured educational journey.
