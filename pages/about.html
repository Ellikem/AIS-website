Mission & Philosophy

Mission

Adwenpa International School, founded in 2025 in North-Legon, Accra, is a co-educational institution with a bold vision to nurture confident, creative, and compassionate young people. Though newly established, it is built on strong foundations of academic excellence, cultural pride, and global awareness. The school is committed to equipping students with the skills, values, and character needed to thrive in higher education and succeed in Ghana and beyond.

Vision

Adwenpa International School, founded in 2025 in North-Legon, Accra, is a co-educational institution with a bold vision to nurture confident, creative, and compassionate young people. Though newly established, it is built on strong foundations of academic excellence, cultural pride, and global awareness. The school is committed to equipping students with the skills, values, and character needed to thrive in higher education and succeed in Ghana and beyond.
