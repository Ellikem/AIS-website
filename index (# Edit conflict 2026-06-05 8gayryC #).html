Who are We

Shaping Futures

Adwenpa International School, founded in 2025 in North Legon, Accra, is a forward-thinking training institution focused on vocational, digital, and social media education. It equips learners with practical, industry-relevant skills in areas such as digital literacy, content creation, and career development, preparing them for real-world opportunities in today’s fast-growing digital economy. Through hands-on training, creativity, and modern learning approaches, the school empowers students with the confidence and technical expertise needed to thrive in employment, entrepreneurship, and the global digital space.

Meet The Founder

Driven By Purpose

Driven by a deep passion for youth empowerment and skills development, the founder of Adwenpa International School established the institution with a vision to bridge the gap between education and real-world opportunities. Recognizing the growing demand for vocational, digital, and social media skills in today’s economy, the founder is committed to equipping learners with practical knowledge, creativity, and confidence to succeed in their chosen careers. Guided by purpose, innovation, and a desire to create lasting impact, the founder continues to inspire a culture of excellence, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning within the school community.

Pathways To success

At Adwenpa International School, we offer diverse pathways to success, empowering students to explore their passions and achieve their goals. Our comprehensive programs in technical, digital, and social media education provide students with the skills, knowledge, and confidence needed to thrive in today’s dynamic world. Whether pursuing careers in technology, entrepreneurship, or creative industries, our students are equipped with the tools and support to navigate their unique journeys towards success.
